GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

Clear information before you decide what to watch next.

01What is the Prime Video free trial?

Prime Video is included with an eligible Amazon Prime membership. Where the current UK offer applies, eligible customers can try Amazon Prime with a 30-day free trial.

02Who is eligible for the free trial?

Availability depends on your Amazon account and previous membership history. The sign-up flow will show whether the current offer is available to you before you confirm.

03How long is the free trial?

The current UK offer is described as a 30-day free trial where applicable. Always check the offer details shown for your account, as eligibility and terms can vary.

04What happens after the free trial?

Membership automatically renews into a paid plan unless you cancel before the trial ends. You can manage or cancel your membership through your Amazon account.

05Can I watch Prime Video on my phone or Smart TV?

Prime Video is available on many compatible mobile devices, smart TVs, computers, streaming media players and game consoles. Compatibility varies by device and region.

06Can I download titles to watch offline?

Many subscription titles can be downloaded for offline viewing on supported devices. Download availability can vary by title, device and location.

07Can multiple people use Prime Video?

Prime Video supports individual profiles, so different members of a household can have their own recommendations and watchlists. The number of simultaneous streams and supported features can vary.

08Does every title come with Prime?

No. Prime Video includes a selection of titles with Prime. Other movies, shows, channels, rentals, purchases or add-on subscriptions may cost extra.

09Does Prime Video contain ads?

The viewing experience and advertising options can vary by plan, title, device and market. Check the current UK membership details for the latest information before subscribing.
