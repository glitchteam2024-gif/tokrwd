Assign a Link to Someone

The tracking code is the routing key, not a label — it is what tells /c/ whose affiliate link this click gets, so it must be the campid on their ad links and it must be unique to them. Their offer link never appears in any page's source; the visitor only ever sees our own redirector.

This assigns an existing landing page to a person — it does not create one. A brand new lander is still a commit (the folder, plus its offer binding and prelander root).

Already-running visitors keep the old route. The Carrd embed caches the answer from /r in sessionStorage for the length of a session, so a re-assignment reaches a mid-session visitor only once their session ends.
