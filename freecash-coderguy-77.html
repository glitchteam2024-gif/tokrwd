4 rules so your games count

1. Start every game from inside Freecash, not straight from the app store.

2. Only pick games you've never installed.

3. Keep ad tracking on (iPhone or Android) and open the game after it installs. If the install can't be verified, it won't pay.

4. Turn off any VPN or ad blocker before you install.

Stuck on “Verifying installation”? Tap Troubleshoot on that screen. It checks the install is new, ad tracking is on and no VPN or ad blocker is running. Tracking can be slow, so give it up to 2 days before contacting support.

When can I cash out? The “Your first Cashout” bar on the Rewards tab shows how close you are to the minimum.

Some games stay locked until you've started others, and your list may be different from anyone else's.
